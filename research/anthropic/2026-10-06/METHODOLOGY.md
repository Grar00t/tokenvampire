# Anthropic / Claude public-source study - 2026-10-06

## What was done
Public original posts, company incident notices, independent research and financial reporting were reviewed. Twelve issue IDs and twelve distinct public author handles were recorded. These are deliberately selected records, not an exhaustive or statistically representative sample. Public account identity and payment claims were not independently verified. No customer funds were recovered, no company systems were tested and no complaint was filed.

## Strongest technical evidence
Anthropic's April 23, 2026 incident report acknowledges three separate changes affecting Claude Code, the Agent SDK and Cowork. It says the API/inference layer was unaffected. It links repeated cache misses caused by a context-management bug to faster-than-expected usage-limit drain, says fixes were complete by April 20, and records a subscriber-limit reset. This does not quantify actual monetary loss.

## Complaint-count limitations
Trustpilot latest indexed snapshots: claude.ai 2029 reviews / 80% one star; anthropic.com 499 / 88% one star. These are all-topic reviews, not counts of verified token complaints. Search/open responses were not synchronized: an older opened anthropic.com snapshot had 415 reviews. A duplicated-looking author/post occurs across the two profiles. Positive reviews also exist. Do not sum profiles as unique people or turn review percentages into customer failure rates. BBB figures and anthropics.com photography software were excluded.

## Finance
Historical 2025 revenue (nearly USD4.6B) is reported by Reuters from a confidential prospectus, not independently audited here. Company-announced May 2026 revenue run rate crossed USD47B; later Reuters anonymous-source reporting put end-July run rate above USD65B. A run rate is a dated annualized pace, not completed annual revenue. The USD965B Series H valuation is not cash, profit or net assets. The USD65B Series H raise is financing. The reported USD20.28B cash/equivalents/short-term-investment balance is dated 2025-12-31, not current liquidity.

## Financial model
Exposure is an ASSUMPTION-ONLY sensitivity: revenue basis multiplied by a user-set share. No verified affected revenue segment, aggregate overcharge, net consumer loss or legal refund entitlement is established. Alternate time bases must not be added.
Audit ROI is an illustrative buyer cash return: (incremental realized/avoided cash benefit minus all audit/implementation costs) divided by those costs. No productivity-time benefit, funding, share valuation, or overlapping refund is counted. Zero denominators yield NOT_DEFINED, never a fabricated zero.

## Next research step

Match provider usage records and invoices to original tasks, outcomes, retries and actual remedies. Distinguish task failure, service unavailability, user/fixture errors and legitimate plan limits. Deduplicate people/accounts/incidents and obtain an independent adjudication sample. Only then estimate segment-specific incidence and net financial impact, with uncertainty and selection limitations.
