## Saudi legal / governance basis

7. **Saudi E-Commerce Law — Bureau of Experts at the Council of Ministers**  
   https://laws.boe.gov.sa/BoeLaws/Laws/LawDetails/360de590-0286-4fa5-a243-aa9100c31979/1  
   Covers electronic commerce and electronic services. The law includes pre-contract information requirements such as core service characteristics, total price/charges and payment/execution arrangements; it also requires an itemized invoice after contract formation.

8. **Saudi Ministry of Commerce — electronically provided services are covered by the E-Commerce Law**  
   https://mc.gov.sa/ar/mediacenter/News/Pages/10-07-19-01.aspx  
   Ministry summary states electronic services are covered by the law; it also describes consumer refund/cancellation rights subject to statutory conditions and exceptions and prohibition of deceptive or misleading electronic advertising.

9. **Saudi PDPL / National Data Governance Platform**  
   https://dgp.sdaia.gov.sa/wps/portal/pdp/knowledgecenter/details/GPDPL  
   Relevant when personal-data processing is involved.

## Evidence rules used by TokenVampire

- Missing data = unknown, never zero.
- Credit top-up is not the same thing as measured spend.
- A usage-limit reset is not automatically a cash refund.
- A cryptographic hash proves identity/integrity of a file version; it does not prove the truth of every claim inside it.
- No LLM should be the sole judge of a material audit finding.
